About us

A small property company in Greenford

GENX PROPERTIES LTD was incorporated in England and Wales on 19 August 2020, under company number 12823021. The company is based in Greenford, West London.

What we do is simple. The company buys residential property in its own name, owns it and lets it to tenants. It is independently owned and run by its director, with no parent company or wider group behind it.

Because the company is the owner of the homes it lets, tenants deal with their landlord directly.

What we do

Buying, owning and letting our own homes

Three connected activities, all carried out by the company on its own account.

Buying residential property

The company acquires residential property in its own name, as a long-term investment rather than on behalf of clients. From time to time it may also sell property it owns.

Owning for the long term

Property the company buys is held as a long-term asset. As a small, owner-run business, decisions about each home are made within the company itself.

Letting to tenants

We let our own residential property to tenants. When you rent from GENX PROPERTIES LTD, your landlord is the company that owns your home. Available homes are not listed on this website.
